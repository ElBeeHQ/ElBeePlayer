The libraries are built unmodified, without the GPL or non-free parts, for macOS 15 and later (arm64 and x86_64),
with this configuration:
  --disable-everything --disable-autodetect --disable-programs --disable-doc --disable-debug --disable-avdevice
  --disable-avfilter --disable-swscale --disable-swresample --enable-shared --disable-static
  --install-name-dir=@rpath --disable-x86asm --enable-zlib --enable-securetransport
  --enable-demuxer=matroska,mov,mpegts,hls,aac,ac3,eac3 --enable-parser=hevc,h264,aac,ac3
  --enable-decoder=aac,ac3,eac3 --enable-bsf=aac_adtstoasc,extract_extradata
  --enable-protocol=file,http,https,tls,tcp,crypto
They are included as separate dynamic frameworks (ElBee Player.app/Contents/Frameworks/libavformat.framework,
libavcodec.framework and libavutil.framework), so you can replace them with your own build of the same FFmpeg
